## App Store Optimization (ASO) Notes

**Key differentiators to emphasize:**
- "No subscription" — Huge selling point, repeat in screenshots
- "No tracking" — Privacy-conscious users appreciate this
- "Family recipes" — Emotional hook, differentiation from generic recipe apps
- "AI scanning" — Tech feature that saves time
- "Beautiful UI" — Show, don't tell (through screenshots)

**Competitor keywords to target:**
- recipe keeper, recipe scanner, family cookbook, recipe organizer, handwritten recipes, recipe digitizer, recipe box, grandma recipes, mom recipes

**Avoid:**
- Generic terms like "best recipe app"
- Overloaded feature lists
- Technical jargon
